Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow

Why Hire a Professional for Storm Damage Repair?

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. Our technicians trace where the water traveled using thermal imaging plus moisture meters, so no affected area is overlooked.
