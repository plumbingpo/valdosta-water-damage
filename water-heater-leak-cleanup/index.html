Dealing With Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Valdosta

The tank on a water heater stores 40 to 80 gallons, yet a leak can release far more than that. The tank is connected to your supply line, so when the bottom rusts through it refills as fast as it empties. Until someone closes the cold water valve, it keeps running. Where the unit sits decides the damage: a garage floor, a hallway closet or, worst of all, an attic above finished rooms.

After extracting the water, our Valdosta crew checks the materials that sat next to the unit. Closet walls and the platform under the tank are usually soaked at the bottom. When the heater is in the garage, items kept on the slab get wet and so does the wall adjoining the house. From an attic it comes down through insulation and ceilings. We measure each area and dry it with air movers and dehumidifiers.

First Five Minutes

What to Do Right Now

  1. Cut the Power or GasElectric units get shut down at the breaker. On a gas unit, rotate the gas control to its off setting. Running the burner or the element with no water in the tank is something to avoid every time.
  2. Shut the Cold Water ValveThe cold line over the tank has a valve, and it closes with a clockwise turn. If it is stuck or leaking, shut off the main water valve for the house.
  3. Beware of Scalding WaterTank water is hot enough to scald. Keep children and pets away, and do not wade through pooled water until it has had time to cool.
  4. Drain the Tank SafelyA garden hose on the drain valve, if you can get to it safely, lets you send what is left in the tank outside or into a floor drain instead of across the floor.
  5. Clear the FloorGet rugs, boxes and other absorbent things up off the wet floor. Cardboard and fabric soak up water quickly and hold it against walls and concrete.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • A drain valve that loosens, cracks or no longer closes all the way.
  • Years of sediment buildup and corrosion wearing a hole in the base of an old steel tank.
  • Flex connectors or inlet and outlet fittings at the top of the heater that have corroded.
  • An overflow pan that cannot drain, because its line is clogged or the pan itself has rusted.
  • A worn anode rod that no longer protects the tank lining from rust.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Items Stored Near the Heater

Whatever is kept by a closet or garage heater, from keepsakes to furniture and boxes, is the first thing to get wet. Cardboard falls apart, and wet contents can mold if they are not dried or discarded quickly.

Ceilings Under an Attic Heater

A heater in the attic sends water through insulation onto the ceiling below. Wet insulation stops insulating and its weight can bring ceiling drywall down.

Shared Garage Wall

As water runs across the garage slab, it wets the drywall and bottom plate of the wall the garage shares with the house and continues into flooring on the other side.

Heater Closet Drywall

Drywall surrounding a heater closet inside the house wicks water up and lets it through to the neighboring rooms. Swollen baseboards and a wall bottom that is still damp within are typical.

Insurance

Insurance and Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Water damage to belongings, walls and floors from a tank that fails suddenly is often covered as a sudden and accidental loss. Usually the unit is left out of the claim, as age related wear is viewed as a maintenance matter. If an adjuster finds signs of a long-term leak, such as rust trails or old staining, the claim can be reduced or denied because gradual leaks usually are not covered. Read your policy, take photos of the damage and the heater, and do not dispose of the old unit before the claim is settled.

What It Costs

What Drives the Price

  • Whether the platform or subfloor under the tank has to be rebuilt before a new heater goes in.
  • If water got to the flooring of adjoining rooms, and the kind of flooring involved.
  • Quantity of insulation and drywall, on walls or ceilings, that must come out.
  • Placement of the unit, as leaks from an attic or an inside closet run higher than those in a garage.
