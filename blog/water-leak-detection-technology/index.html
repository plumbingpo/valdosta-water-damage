Modern Leak Detection for Homes in Valdosta, GA

Home / Blog / Water Leak Detection Technology in Valdosta, GA

For homeowners in Valdosta, GA, today's leak detection devices add a highly useful line of defense against water damage. These devices can detect leaks in their earliest stages, often before visible damage occurs, and alert you immediately, potentially saving thousands in restoration costs. Read on for a look at current options in leak detection and how they protect a home.

Types of Leak Detection Systems

Water leak detection ranges from simple standalone sensors to comprehensive whole-home systems. A point sensor is a small battery-powered unit that sits near a sink, washing machine, water heater, or other risk area and sounds an alarm if it detects water. With a smart sensor on your WiFi, your phone gets an alert as soon as moisture is detected, whether you are home or away. A whole-home system watches the main water line for unusual flow and can shut the water off automatically once it detects a leak.

Where to Place Leak Detectors

For maximum protection, place leak detection sensors in these high-risk locations: near the water heater, behind the washing machine, under the kitchen sink, under bathroom sinks, near the dishwasher, near the refrigerator (if it has a water line), in the basement or near the sump pump, near HVAC condensate drain lines, and in crawl spaces or utility areas with plumbing.

Leak Detection by a Professional

Suspected leaks that leave no visible trace call for professionals, who use advanced technology to pinpoint where water is getting in. Moisture under a floor or behind a wall changes the surface temperature, and thermal imaging cameras show that difference. Acoustic listening equipment picks up the noise water makes as it moves through concealed pipes. For precise moisture content numbers in building materials, technicians rely on moisture meters.
