Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Valdosta, GA

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Valdosta, GA

Nobody controls the weather, yet Valdosta, GA property owners can still get ahead of flood damage with a few proactive steps. A prepared home takes far less of a hit when it floods, and the savings in restoration costs can reach the thousands. This guide from Valdosta Restoration covers the most effective preparation strategies for Valdosta homeowners.

Find Out How Flood-Prone You Are

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. FEMA publishes flood maps that show whether a designated flood zone includes your property. Even if you're not in a high-risk zone, consider that most flood claims come from outside designated flood zones. Look at how your land slopes, how it drains, and how close it sits to any waterway that might overflow in heavy rain.

Physical Steps to Protect Your Home

Key physical preparations include ensuring proper grading slopes away from your foundation, keeping gutters and downspouts clear and extending discharge at least 6 feet from the foundation, installing and maintaining a sump pump with battery backup, sealing foundation cracks and applying waterproof coatings, elevating electrical panels and HVAC equipment above potential flood levels, and storing important documents and valuables above ground level.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

A typical homeowner's insurance policy excludes damage from rising floodwater. Consider purchasing flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier, and since there is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins, don't wait for a forecast to act. For insurance purposes, take video and photos of the property and what you own. Know what your deductible is and how high your coverage limits go, and keep the documents within easy reach.

Plan Your Emergency Response

Have a plan in place before flooding threatens. Pack a go-bag and learn the routes you would take to evacuate. Keep Valdosta Restoration's number ((843) 439-8814) in your phone for immediate post-flood professional cleanup. The contact information for your insurance agent should be within quick reach as well. If flooding is forecast, move valuables to upper levels, turn off utilities if instructed, and document your property's condition before the event.
